# Mena Karam

Research portfolio in robotic perception, 3D computer vision, geometric reconstruction, and robot control.

Current interests include physically grounded perception, 3D reconstruction from challenging visual data, mapping and spatial representation, robotic locomotion and manipulation, and the interaction between sensing quality and downstream robotic decision-making.

## Selected areas

- Robotic perception and 3D computer vision
- Image restoration and physically grounded imaging models
- Multi-view geometry and 3D reconstruction
- Robot kinematics, locomotion, and control
- ROS-based manipulation and system integration

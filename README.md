# Mena Karam

Robot Perception and 3D computer vision | M.Sc. Automation and Robotics, TU Dortmund | Dortmund, Germany

My work focuses on Physically-grounded visual perception, modeling how an image is formed (optics, defocus and motion blur, sensor limits) and integrating such a model in 3D reconstruction and robotic perception. Currently applying for PhD positions in robotic perception and 3D vision. 

My research interest lies at the intersection of robotic perception, geometric reconstruction, and robot control. In particular, model-based and learning-based robotic perception methods.
